## Full description (max 4000)

Chartoon teaches trading the way Duolingo teaches languages: a few minutes a day, bite-sized lessons, XP and rewards, and Shamak, a friendly candlestick mascot who's with you all the way.

📚 8 COURSES, 279 SHORT LESSONS
Start from "what is a market?" and build up to real skills: candlesticks, trends, support and resistance, indicators (RSI, MACD, moving averages), chart patterns, Fibonacci, price action, supply and demand, smart money concepts, Elliott waves, harmonics, risk management and trading psychology. For forex, gold and crypto.

🎯 INTERACTIVE, NOT JUST READING
Tap the right candle, drag a support line onto the chart, place your stop loss, predict where price goes next, match terms and fill in the blanks. Mistakes come back later for review, so they stick.

📈 TRADING SIMULATOR WITH LIVE PRICES
A full-screen chart like MetaTrader with timeframes, indicators and 42 drawing tools (trend lines, channels, Fibonacci, harmonic and Elliott tools). Place market, limit and stop orders with stop loss and take profit, pick your leverage, then learn from your trade journal, statistics and a coach that points out your mistakes. Replay past markets and take on simulator challenges. All with virtual money.

⚔️ CHARTOON DUELS
Three rounds against Shamak or a friend: a speed quiz, a real-chart prediction and a 60-second trade.

🔥 A HABIT THAT STICKS
Daily streaks, a daily goal, quests, reward chests and a weekly league with promotion and relegation keep you coming back.

💬 COMMUNITY AND AN AI ASSISTANT
Share analyses in chat groups (with blocking and reporting), and ask Shamak, the AI assistant, about your lessons and your simulator trades.

🏆 SHARE YOUR PROGRESS
Turn a finished course, a streak record or a winning trade into a card and share it with friends.

🌍 ENGLISH AND PERSIAN
The whole app, every lesson included, is available in English and Persian (Farsi). Sign in with email or with a mobile number from over 100 countries, or learn as a guest without an account.
